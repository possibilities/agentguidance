Classify along two axes:

- **Kind** — question/answer, report (synthesize from local sources), deep
  research (external sources, cited), or actionable work.
- **Size** (work only) — **small**: fits one or two files, no
  schema/protocol/UX contract change, direction obvious. **Substantial**:
  multi-module, introduces a contract, or reads as three or more independently
  sequenceable moves. When torn, treat it as substantial — a sketch round is
  cheaper than backing out a wrong change.
