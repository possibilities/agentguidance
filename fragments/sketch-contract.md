The sketch is short enough to inspect in one read:

- `## Goal` — 1–2 sentences on what the work is for.
- `## Direction` — 3–6 bullets: the chosen approach, each with a clause of why
  when the reasoning isn't obvious. Commit to one direction; a near-miss
  alternative is a single bullet under risks, nowhere else.
- `## Touchpoints` — concrete files, modules, and commands, citing `path:line`
  where useful, saying what changes at each.
- `## Risks & unknowns` — at most 4 bullets, one item each.
- `## Open decisions` — at most 2 questions, each with a stated default.
  Omit the section when nothing is open.
